Don't Know Your Brand?

If your cart has no visible logo or brand badge, check under the seat or on the frame rail for a serial number plate. Most manufacturers stamp the brand name and model year there. Common off-brands include Bintelli, Evolution EV, Icon EV, Advanced EV, Gorilla Rides EV, Star EV, and Tomberlin. All use standard golf cart dimensions and work with universal accessories.
